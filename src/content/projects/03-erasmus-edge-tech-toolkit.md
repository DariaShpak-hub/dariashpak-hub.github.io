Complete editorial layout and book cover design for the Erasmus+ EDGE Tech Toolkit publication, produced for the Games & Society Lab at Uppsala Universitet.

## Scope

- Cover concept and execution
- Full interior layout (long-document production)
- Typography system
- Print-ready file preparation

## About the Project

Designed for readability, structured hierarchy, and educational usability. The publication targets educators and researchers in technology-enhanced learning environments.

The cover features a layered composition combining classical sculpture with digital imagery — visualising the tension between tradition and emerging technology. Interior layouts balance dense academic content with clear typographic structure.

Digital identity and structured content system for Swedish Hub — a volunteer organisation supporting Ukrainian refugees integrating into Swedish society.

## Scope

- Visual identity framework
- Modular templates
- Multilingual hierarchy
- Content structure guidelines

## About the Project

The system ensures accessible, consistent communication across digital platforms. The challenge: content spans two languages (Ukrainian and Swedish) with varying text lengths, requiring a template system flexible enough to accommodate both while maintaining visual coherence.

The design supports educational content about Swedish regions, cultural integration topics, and community resources — published consistently across an active Instagram presence.

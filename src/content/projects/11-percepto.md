Visual communication for Percepto — a technology company producing autonomous drone-in-a-box inspection solutions.

## Scope

- Website visuals
- Product presentation assets
- Marketing imagery
- Product photo editing

## About the Project

Balanced technical precision with commercial clarity. Percepto's hardware required imagery that communicated industrial-grade capability while remaining accessible to enterprise buyers across sectors.

Product photography and post-production focused on accurate material representation, clean backgrounds, and contextual staging — supporting both technical documentation and marketing use cases.

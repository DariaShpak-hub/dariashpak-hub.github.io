Visual identity and catalogue design for *MUD in the Machine* — an artistic research project by Josephine Rydberg, produced with Stockholm University of the Arts (SKH) and Region Gävleborg.

## About the Project

The project balances experimental aesthetics with institutional clarity. The visual system draws on collage, architectural perspective grids, and organic texture to express the intersection of digital and physical experience.

Materials needed to serve both artistic and academic documentation purposes — functioning as a formal record while conveying the experimental nature of the work.

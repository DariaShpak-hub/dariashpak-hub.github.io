Lifestyle visualisation and promotional materials for Telbix — an interior lighting brand.

## Scope

- Material accuracy
- Lighting realism
- Architectural context
- Catalogue-level presentation

## About the Project

Each product was visualised within realistic interior environments — bedrooms, dining rooms, living spaces — designed to support product credibility in commercial settings.

The work balances technical precision with aspirational lifestyle aesthetics. Lighting products require particular care: material rendering, light behaviour, and spatial context must all read convincingly for the imagery to support purchasing decisions.

Conceptual advertising campaign for Daniel Wellington — focused on minimal luxury positioning and product-centred storytelling.

## Scope

- Campaign visuals
- Product-focused storytelling
- High-end retouching direction

## About the Project

The visual language emphasises restraint, clarity, and product centrality. Campaign materials were developed across multiple formats — from Valentine's Day gift sets to watch-in-motion hero imagery — each maintaining the brand's minimal luxury aesthetic.

The work explores how premium positioning communicates through negative space, material quality, and typographic precision rather than decoration.

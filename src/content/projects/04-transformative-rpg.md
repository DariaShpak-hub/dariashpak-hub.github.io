Book cover and full interior layout for *Transformative Role-Playing Game Design* — a research publication edited by Sarah Lynne Bowman, Elektra Diakolambrianou, and Simon Brind, published through Uppsala Universitets förlag.

## Scope

- Cover design (Parts I & II)
- Interior layout and typography
- Print-ready file preparation

## About the Project

The publication explores transformative RPG practice — encompassing therapeutic, leisurely, and educational applications. The cover illustration depicts figures in a cosmic, painterly composition that captures the transformative potential of role-play.

The design needed to serve both academic credibility and genuine visual appeal, reaching practitioners, researchers, and educators simultaneously.

Digital visual system developed for Evocative Games — a research initiative at Uppsala Universitet focused on game studies and research dissemination.

## Scope

- Digital presentations
- Interface mockups
- Research dissemination materials

## About the Project

A structured graphic language was built from the ground up to serve the project's communication needs across multiple formats. The system uses a distinctive dark palette with gradient purples, communicating the intersection of play, research, and digital culture.

The identity balances experimental aesthetics with academic credibility — ensuring materials work in both conference and digital publishing contexts.

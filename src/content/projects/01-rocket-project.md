Visual identity system developed for the ROCKET project — an Erasmus+ Cooperation Partnership focused on role-playing games, critical virtual exchange, and conflict transformation.

## Scope

- Logo system and variations
- Conference materials
- Website graphics
- Institutional co-branding alignment

## About the Project

The identity was developed to function across multiple universities and communication platforms while maintaining coherence and clarity. Project partners include Austin Community College, Uppsala Universitet, Georg-August-Universität Göttingen, and University of Groningen.

The visual language needed to feel academic yet dynamic — capable of representing both digital and physical touchpoints across different institutional contexts.

Packaging and marketing design for two art and stationery brands — Colozoo (brush pens and markers) and Lobome (sketchbooks and stickers) — developed for competitive shelf presence and digital marketplaces.

## About the Project

The visual systems were developed with Amazon listings and retail shelf presence as the primary contexts. Colozoo uses vibrant, energetic colour to communicate creative potential, while Lobome takes a more refined, product-forward approach.

Each product line required its own visual logic, but both needed to communicate clearly at small sizes and in dense marketplace environments where visual differentiation drives purchase decisions.
